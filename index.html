How it works, and honest limits

Pipeline

  • Chunking: ~120-word passages, 30-word overlap
  • Embeddings: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (23 MB), cosine similarity top-3
  • Answer: DistilBERT extractive QA (65 MB) over the best passage
  • Models download once from Hugging Face CDN, then run offline from the browser cache

Limits

  • Extractive, not generative: answers are exact spans of your text, not fluent summaries
  • First question downloads about 90 MB of models (needs a connection once)
  • English documents work best; numbers and dates can confuse the QA model
  • Documents live in memory only - reloading the page clears them
  • If the answer is not in your documents, it says so instead of inventing one
